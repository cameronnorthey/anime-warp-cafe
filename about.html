About Anime Warp Café

Built by people who couldn't pick just one favorite show.

Anime Warp Café started as a one-weekend pop-up with a single theme and a folding table of decorations. It sold out in four hours. Instead of picking a permanent theme, we picked a permanent gimmick instead — the room itself, that changes worlds every month.

One lease, endless worlds

Decor, playlist, and a handful of themed specials all rotate on the first of the month — so the same four walls can be a cyberpunk arcade in March and a garden café in April.

Food first, theme second

Every dish on the Portal Menu Builder has to hold up as food on its own before it earns a trope name. The theming is the bonus, never the substitute for a good plate.

A regular's spot

We're built for the group that reschedules three times to all show up together — long tables, shareable plates, and a room that's easy to photograph without asking.

How We Pick the Next World

The monthly theme isn't random.

A few different things pull the vote in different directions, and we try to actually listen to all of them:

Guest vote

Every receipt includes a code to vote for next month's world. The top few contenders make the shortlist the staff picks from.

Seasonal timing

We try to line up with whatever's airing that season, so the theme feels current instead of a rerun of last year's favorite.

What we can actually cook

Some worlds are easier to turn into a menu than others. A trope has to translate into real dishes, not just a color palette on the walls.

"We didn't want to be the café that does one anime theme forever and then feels dated in two years. Changing the world every month means we get to keep building instead of maintaining." — Emmet, founder, Anime Warp Café
